About Me

Musician, Drummer & Programmer

My name is Fidele Murengezi. I am a passionate musician and technology enthusiast with a strong interest in creativity, learning and personal development. I completed my Ordinary Level studies at GS Ntanza B and continued my Advanced Level education at Agahozo-Shalom Youth Village (ASYV). During my Advanced Level studies, I developed my musical abilities and learned to play different musical instruments, including the guitar and piano. I also improved my drumming skills and developed a deeper understanding of rhythm and music. Apart from music, I am interested in programming and technology. I enjoy creating websites, learning programming languages and exploring how technology can solve real-world problems. I believe in continuous learning, teamwork, discipline and using my talents to inspire others.
