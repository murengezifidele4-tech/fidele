ABOUT ME

My name is Fidele, also known as Fidele the Drummer. I am a passionate drummer, musician, and creative individual who believes in the power of music to bring people together. Music is more than just a hobby to me; it is a way of expressing myself, communicating emotions, and inspiring others.

My musical journey has helped me develop discipline, patience, creativity, teamwork, and confidence. As a drummer, I enjoy exploring different rhythms, improving my techniques, and collaborating with other musicians to create beautiful musical experiences.

Regarding my education, I completed my Ordinary Level studies at GS Ntanza B, where I built the foundation of my academic journey.

I continued my Advanced Level studies at Agahozo-Shalom Youth Village (ASYV), an environment that has contributed to my personal, academic, and leadership development.

Beyond music and education, I enjoy learning new skills, working with others, taking on challenges, and discovering opportunities that allow me to grow. My goal is to continue developing as a musician, sharing my talent with the world, and making a positive impact through music.
